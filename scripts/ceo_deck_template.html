4 Step-by-Step Experience: From Question to Verified Legal Action
1
Natural Language Search (Just Like Google or WhatsApp)
The user opens the clean web portal and types a question naturally into the central search bar: "What is the notice period required to terminate an employee under the Industrial Disputes Act?" or "Can our company enforce a non-compete clause after resignation?". They can also click one of the quick sample query pills for instant results. Zero technical syntax or legal jargon required.
2
Instant Synthesized Answer with Traffic Light Badges GREEN: Verified YELLOW: Caution RED: Escalated
The platform delivers a plain-English synthesized summary. Right next to it is the confidence light:
• 🟢 GREEN LIGHT: 100% verified against statutory law or company contracts. Safe to rely on immediately.
• 🟡 YELLOW LIGHT: Helpful preliminary guidance, but recommends checking with senior counsel before signing.
• 🔴 RED LIGHT: The system refuses to make up fake answers. It automatically routes the query to the Admin Desk!
3
Exact Clause-Level Proof (Zero "AI Hallucination" or Guesswork)
Unlike public chatbots that make up fake cases, every answer here is anchored to verifiable source cards showing the exact Act Name (e.g. Industrial Disputes Act, 1947), the precise section (e.g. Section 25F), and verbatim quotes. Clicking "Inspect Full Document" opens the original statute modal with highlighted clauses.
4
Outdated Law Safety Net (Protecting the Company from Major Errors)
If a user queries an Act or clause that has been amended, repealed, or overturned by the Supreme Court (e.g. Section 66A IT Act), the app displays a prominent Red Warning Banner alerting them that this section was struck down in Shreya Singhal v. UOI. This actively protects leadership from non-compliance liabilities.
5
Smart Video Seeking (Skip to the Exact Second in 1-Hour Webinars)
Recorded legal webinars and HR policy lectures are fully indexed with second-by-second timestamps. When a search matches a point taught in a video, a "Play at Timestamp [04:20]" button appears. Clicking it launches the video player and seeks directly to that exact second, saving 40+ minutes of scrubbing through video timelines.
6
Personal Research Vault & 1-Click Executive Brief Export
Users can save important clauses into their private vault with one click on "Bookmark Clause". For meetings with leadership or external vendors, clicking "Export Legal Brief" generates a clean, formatted executive brief ready to print or circulate.
